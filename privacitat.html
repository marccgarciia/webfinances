Finances no recull res. Sense comptes, sense analítica, sense telemetria, sense informes d'errors, sense publicitat i sense rastreig de cap mena. L'app només fa servir la xarxa si tu actives la sincronització opcional amb iCloud, i aleshores només parla amb el teu propi iCloud.

Quines dades es recullen

Cap. Finances no té registre, perfil d'usuari ni servidor al darrere. No llegeix els teus contactes, la teva ubicació, les teves fotos, el teu micròfon ni els teus identificadors, i no inclou cap sistema d'analítica ni de publicitat. No hi ha recollida per descriure, perquè no hi ha recollida.

És la mateixa resposta que es dona al qüestionari de privacitat de l'App Store, on totes les categories figuren com a «Dades no recollides».

Res d'això s'envia, es transmet ni es comparteix amb nosaltres ni amb ningú més. L'única excepció és la sincronització amb iCloud, que només funciona si tu l'actives (vegeu més avall).

  • Bloqueig amb Face ID, Touch ID o codi. El fa l'iOS. L'app només rep un «sí» o un «no»: mai veu la teva cara, la teva empremta ni el teu codi.
  • Avisos. Els recordatoris de pagaments i pressupostos són notificacions locals programades al teu iPhone. No passen per cap servidor.

Sincronització amb iCloud (opcional)

Està desactivada per defecte. Només si l'actives a Perfil, l'app desa una còpia de les teves dades al teu espai privat d'iCloud per mantenir-les al dia als dispositius on facis servir el mateix ID d'Apple.

  • Què se sincronitza. Els teus moviments, comptes, categories, pressupostos i pagaments recurrents, i alguns ajustos (divisa, nom, objectiu d'estalvi i aspecte de la targeta d'Inici).
  • Qui pot veure-les. Només tu, amb el teu compte d'Apple. Aquestes dades viuen al teu compte d'iCloud, no a cap servidor nostre: nosaltres no hi podem accedir, no les rebem i no les compartim.
  • Qui les gestiona. Apple, mitjançant iCloud, segons les seves pròpies condicions i la seva política de privacitat. És l'únic tercer implicat.
  • Canvis en diversos dispositius. Amb la sincronització activada, el que esborris o restauris en un dispositiu es reflecteix als altres.
  • Apagar-la. Les teves dades continuen al dispositiu. La còpia que ja hi hagi a iCloud no s'esborra sola: la pots eliminar des dels ajustos d'iCloud del teu compte d'Apple.

Tercers

No inclou codi d'analítica, publicitat ni atribució de tercers i no envia res a cap servei extern, tret que tu ho activis, al teu propi iCloud. L'únic tercer implicat és Apple, per a les compres i, si actives la sincronització, per a iCloud (vegeu més avall).

Privacy Policy

Finances for iPhone · Effective 9 November 2026

Finances collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app only uses the network if you turn on the optional iCloud sync, and then it only talks to your own iCloud.

What data is collected

None. Finances has no sign-in, no user profile and no server behind it. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or identifiers, and it contains no analytics or advertising frameworks. There is no collection to describe, because there is no collection.

This is the same answer given in the App Store privacy questionnaire, where every category is marked «Data Not Collected».

Where your data is kept

By default, your entries, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring payments and goals are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent, transmitted or shared with us or anybody else. The only exception is iCloud sync, which works only if you turn it on (see below).

  • Lock with Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. iOS does it. The app only receives a «yes» or a «no»: it never sees your face, your fingerprint or your passcode.
  • Reminders. Payment and budget alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They do not go through any server.
  • Widget. It reads a small summary that the app leaves in a shared space on your own iPhone so it can be shown on the Home Screen.

iCloud sync (optional)

It is off by default. Only if you turn it on in Profile does the app keep a copy of your data in your private iCloud space, so it stays up to date on the devices where you use the same Apple Account.

  • What is synced. Your entries, accounts, categories, budgets and recurring payments, plus a few settings (currency, name, savings goal and the look of the Home card).
  • Who can see it. Only you, with your Apple Account. That data lives in your iCloud account, not on a server of ours: we cannot access it, we do not receive it and we do not share it.
  • Who handles it. Apple, through iCloud, under its own terms and privacy policy. It is the only third party involved.
  • Changes across devices. With sync on, what you delete or restore on one device is reflected on the others.
  • Turning it off. Your data stays on the device. The copy already in iCloud is not deleted automatically: you can remove it from the iCloud settings of your Apple Account.

Third parties

The app shares no data with anyone, because it collects none. It bundles no third-party analytics, advertising or attribution code and sends nothing to any external service, except, if you turn it on, to your own iCloud. The only third party involved is Apple, for purchases and, if you turn on sync, for iCloud (see below).

What is stored on your device, and for how long

All of the above, plus your preferences (language, currency, name, theme and card settings), is kept for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes everything on the device; if you turned on iCloud, the copy in your iCloud is kept until you remove it.

Important: because no copy exists on any server of ours, if you delete the app without exporting a copy first and without having iCloud sync on, we cannot recover your data. Backups made by your iPhone itself (to iCloud or to a computer) are handled by Apple.

Copies and files you export

«Export data» creates a file with all your data, and «Import entries» reads a CSV file you choose. These files are not encrypted and go exactly where you decide to save or send them. Keep them somewhere safe and share them only with people you choose. We never receive them.

This website

This website is static and has no accounts. To display, it loads styles, fonts and icons from third-party servers (Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts and cdnjs/Cloudflare), which may receive your IP address and technical browser details when serving them. We use no cookies, analytics or tracking. We store in your own browser (local storage) only your language and theme preference; that information never leaves your device.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) and, like every web server, it records the technical details of each request (IP address, time, page requested, browser version) to serve and protect the site. We do not use those logs to build profiles and we do not combine them with anything. That is the only processing this website does, and its lawful basis is our legitimate interest in delivering and securing the pages (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Writing to us

If you write to holafinancesapp@gmail.com, we process what you send (your email address, your name if you sign it, and whatever you tell us) for the single purpose of answering you. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us and, where your message concerns a purchase, the performance of that relationship (article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), which stores the messages on our behalf.

Support messages are kept for twelve months from the last reply and are then deleted, unless a message forms part of a legal claim, in which case it is kept until the claim is closed. Nothing you write is used for marketing or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data and withdrawing consent

The app holds no personal data of yours that you could ask us to access, correct, export or erase, because it collects none and nothing reaches us. To remove what is on your iPhone, delete the app; if you turned on iCloud, also remove the copy from the iCloud settings of your Apple Account. The app asks for no consent, so there is none to withdraw.

Your rights

For the only things that do involve personal data (this site's server log and any email you send us) you have the rights the GDPR gives you: to ask what we hold, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to holafinancesapp@gmail.com and you will have an answer within one month.

There is no automated decision-making and no profiling. If you think your data has been handled badly, you can complain to the Spanish supervisory authority (the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) or to the authority of the EU country you live in.

Purchases

Finances Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple takes the payment, and Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction. The app only asks the system whether a valid purchase exists and receives a «yes» or a «no». We never see your name, your email address, your Apple Account or your payment details.

Children

Nothing is collected from anybody, so nothing is collected from children. The app is not directed at children and has no social, messaging or user-generated content features.
